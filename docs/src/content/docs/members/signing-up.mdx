## Sign up to troop

<Steps>

1. Click **Events** in the top right, then click the event's name.

   <Image src={eventsList} alt="The Events page with an event name circled" />

2. Click **Sign Up to Attend**. If it says **Join Waitlist**, the event is full: see
   [Waitlists](../waitlists/).

   <Image src={eventSignup} alt="The Sign Up to Attend button circled" />

3. Leave **Trooping** selected and click **Continue**.

   <Image src={attendance} alt="Trooping and the Continue button circled" />

4. If you're asked for details like your Legion ID or mobile number, fill them in and click
   **Save and Continue**. You're only asked once. See
   [Your details and costumes](../your-details-and-costumes/).

5. Tick the costumes you're bringing and click **Continue**.

   <Image src={costumes} alt="A costume checkbox circled" />

6. Click **Confirm Signup**.

   <Image src={confirm} alt="The Confirm Signup button circled" />

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">**Sign Up** in the events list skips to step 3.</Aside>

## Sign up to wrangle

Wranglers help out of costume. You don't need to be a full member, and aren't asked for a
Legion ID or costume.

Follow the steps above, choosing **Wrangling** at step 3.

## Multi-day events

To troop some days and wrangle or skip others:

<Steps>

1. At step 3, tick **I am not doing the same thing every day**.

2. Choose **Trooping**, **Wrangling** or **Not attending** for each day.

   <Image src={perDay} alt="A different choice for each day" />

3. Click **Continue**.

</Steps>

## Change your signup

<Steps>

1. Open the event and click **Update Your Signup**.

   <Image src={updateSignup} alt="The Update Your Signup button circled" />

2. Make your changes, click **Continue** through the steps, then click **Save Changes**.

</Steps>

You keep your place in the queue for any role you already had.

## Pull out of an event

<Steps>

1. Open the event and click **Update Your Signup**.

2. Choose **Not attending** and click **Continue**.

   <Image src={notAttending} alt="Not attending circled" />

3. Click **Withdraw Signup**.

</Steps>

If there's a waitlist, the next person gets your place.

## When you can't sign up

| Message                                              | Meaning                |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| **Signups for this event have closed.**              | The cutoff has passed. |
| **This event has finished, so signups have closed.** | The event is over.     |
| **This event is not open for signups.**              | It isn't open yet.     |
| **You must be logged in to sign up.**                | Log in first.          |

<Aside type="caution" title="After signups close">
	You can't change or withdraw your signup. Message the coordinator instead.
</Aside>
