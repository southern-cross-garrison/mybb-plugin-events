When you sign up, you're asked for any of these that aren't on your profile yet. They're
saved, so you only enter them once.

<Image src={prerequisites} alt="The Signup Prerequisites step" />

## What you're asked for

| Detail                | Asked of               | Notes                                                |
| --------------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **Legion ID**         | Troopers               | Just the number: for TK-12345, type `12345`.         |
| **Preferred Name**    | Everyone               | What to call you on the day.                         |
| **Mobile Number**     | Troopers and wranglers | [Why we ask](../contact-details/)                    |
| **Emergency Contact** | Troopers and wranglers | A name and number. [Why we ask](../contact-details/) |
| **WWCC Number**       | Events that need one   | Working With Children Check.                         |

Social events only ask for your preferred name, and a WWCC if needed.

When you type your Legion ID, the form shows whose record it is so you can check it.

<Aside>
	Only coordinators, an event's point of contact and admins can see your mobile number and emergency
	contact. See [Why we ask for contact details](../contact-details/).
</Aside>

## Your costume list

Your costumes come from your 501st Legion record, fetched each time you sign up to troop.
Newly approved costumes appear automatically.

If the Legion's records can't be reached or yours is private, your saved costumes are
used instead. If you have none saved, you're asked to type them, one per line.

### If your costumes are wrong

<Steps>

1. Check the costume is approved on your 501st Legion profile.

2. Check your Legion ID in [**User CP** > **Edit Profile**](https://501scg.org/forum/usercp.php?action=profile).

3. Sign up again, or click **Update Your Signup**.

</Steps>

## Preapproved costumes

If enabled, you can troop in a preapproved costume that isn't on your Legion record yet.

<Steps>

1. On the costume step, type the costume in the **Preapproval:** box.

   <Image src={preapproval} alt="The Preapproval box circled" />

2. If instructions are shown, follow them and tick the box to confirm your preapproval.

3. Click **Continue**.

</Steps>

It's listed as `Preapproval: <costume>`. You confirm again each time you use it.

## Change your details

Go to [**User CP** > **Edit Profile**](https://501scg.org/forum/usercp.php?action=profile). Changes apply to your next signup.
