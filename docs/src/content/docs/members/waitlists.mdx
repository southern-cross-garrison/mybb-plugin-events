## Join a waitlist

When an event is full, click **Join Waitlist** and sign up as usual. The last button reads
**Join the Waitlist**.

<Image src={joinWaitlist} alt="The Join Waitlist button circled" />

If only one role is full, you can take a place in the other straight away.

## Your place in the queue

The event shows **Waitlisted** and your position, such as `#2`. The queue is also listed
under **Who is Attending**.

<Image src={waitlisted} alt="Waitlisted: Trooping (#1)" />

## Getting a place

Places go in signup order. When someone pulls out or the limit is raised, the next person
gets the place and is sent a private message.

If the limit is lowered, the latest signups move back to the front of the waitlist and are
messaged too.

<Aside type="caution">
  You don't have a place until you're told so. If you're still waitlisted close to the
  day, check with the coordinator.
</Aside>

## Multi-day events

Each day has its own queue, so you can have a place one day and be waiting for another.

## Leave the waitlist

Click **Update Your Signup**, choose **Not attending**, and click **Withdraw Signup**.

Waitlisted days aren't added to [your calendar](../calendar/) until you get a place.
