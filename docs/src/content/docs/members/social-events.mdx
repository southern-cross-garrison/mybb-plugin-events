Social events, like dinners and movie nights, are marked **Social** in the events list.

<Image src={socialPill} alt="A Social event circled in the events list" />

They're simpler than troops:

- You sign up as an attendee. No trooping or wrangling, and no costumes.
- You're only asked for your preferred name, and a WWCC if needed.
- For a one-day event, **Sign Up to Attend** goes straight to **Confirm Signup**.
- For a multi-day event, you choose which days you're coming.

[Changing your signup](../signing-up/#change-your-signup) and
[waitlists](../waitlists/) work the same as for troops.

Social events have no troop report and don't count towards [My Troops](../my-troops/).
