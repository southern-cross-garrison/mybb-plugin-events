- **Subscribe** (recommended): every event you sign up for appears in your calendar and
  stays up to date.
- **Add one event**: a one-off copy that doesn't update.

## Subscribe

<Steps>

1. Go to [**User CP** > **Calendar Subscription**](https://501scg.org/forum/usercp.php?action=events_calendar).

2. Click **Make my subscription link**.

   <Image src={feedCreate} alt="Make my subscription link circled" />

3. Add it to your calendar:

   <Tabs>
   <TabItem label="iPhone, Mac, Outlook">

   Click **Subscribe in my calendar app** and confirm in your calendar app.

   <Image src={feedSubscribe} alt="Subscribe in my calendar app circled" />

   </TabItem>
   <TabItem label="Google Calendar">

   1. Click the arrow beside **Subscribe in my calendar app**, choose **Copy calendar
      URL**, and copy the link.
   2. In Google Calendar on a computer, click **+** beside **Other calendars** >
      **From URL**.
   3. Paste the link and click **Add calendar**.

   </TabItem>
   </Tabs>

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="The link is only shown once">
  Subscribe or copy it straight away. If you lose it, make a new one.
</Aside>

Entries show your role, such as **Trooping: Supanova Sydney**. Changes can take up to a day
to appear in Google Calendar; Apple and Outlook update more often.

### Keep your link private

Anyone with the link can see your signups. If it's been shared, click **Make a new link**
on the Calendar Subscription page: the old one stops working. Click **Turn off** to stop
the subscription.

## Add one event

On an event you've signed up for, click **Add to Calendar** and open the downloaded file.

<Image src={addToCalendar} alt="Add to Calendar circled" />

<Aside>
  This copy doesn't update if the event or your signup changes.
</Aside>
