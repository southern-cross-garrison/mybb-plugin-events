Go to [**User CP** > **My Troops**](https://501scg.org/forum/usercp.php?action=events_troops). Only you can see it.

<Image src={myTroops} alt="The My Troops page" />

It shows your troop count as a trooper and wrangler, your last troop, your costumes, a
12-month chart, your full history, and how many members you've out-trooped.

## Missing a troop?

A troop counts once its [troop report](../troop-reports/) is posted and lists you.

- If the event shows **Needs Troop Report**, the report isn't posted yet.
- If you're not in the report, ask whoever posted it to add you.

Social events aren't counted.
