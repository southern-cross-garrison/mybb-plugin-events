Troopers and wranglers are asked for a **mobile number** and an **emergency contact** the
first time they sign up.

<Image src={asked} alt="The signup step asking for a mobile number and emergency contact" />

## Your mobile number

So the coordinator can reach you on the day if:

- the time, meeting point or plans change, or the event is cancelled
- you're not at the meeting point
- you get separated from the group (costumes limit vision and hearing)
- the group needs to regroup or move

## Your emergency contact

Trooping is physical: costumes are hot and heavy, visibility is poor, and you're often on
your feet in a crowd for hours. If you're hurt or unwell, the coordinator needs to reach
someone who knows you.

If you can't speak for yourself, your emergency contact can tell first aiders or
paramedics what they need to know. Choose someone likely to answer, who knows you're
trooping.

<Aside type="tip">
  Tell your emergency contact you've listed them. Keep both up to date in
  [**User CP** > **Edit Profile**](https://501scg.org/forum/usercp.php?action=profile).
</Aside>

## Who can see them

They're not on your profile. They only appear on the **attendance sheet** of events you've
signed up to, which only these people can open:

- the event's coordinator
- the event's point of contact
- the garrison's event coordinators
- forum administrators

Coordinators often bring a printed copy to the event.

<Image
	src={columns}
	alt="The Mobile and Emergency Contact columns circled on an attendance sheet"
/>

Social events don't ask for these details, but show them on the attendance sheet if
they're on your profile.
