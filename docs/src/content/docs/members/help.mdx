## I can't find an event

Check the region filter, and tick **Show archived** if it's already happened. Events
aren't listed until they open for signups.

## There's no signup button

The event shows why instead. See [When you can't sign up](../signing-up/#when-you-cant-sign-up).

## I need to pull out after signups closed

Message the coordinator.

## My costume isn't listed

See [If your costumes are wrong](../your-details-and-costumes/#if-your-costumes-are-wrong).

## Why do you need my mobile number?

See [Why we ask for contact details](../contact-details/).

## Will I be told if I get off the waitlist?

Yes, by private message.

## My calendar didn't update

A single event added with **Add to Calendar** doesn't update; [subscribe](../calendar/#subscribe)
instead. Subscribed calendars can take up to a day to update in Google Calendar.

## A troop is missing from My Troops

See [Missing a troop?](../my-troops/#missing-a-troop)

## I keep getting "Troop Report Needed" messages

The report for an event you trooped hasn't been posted. [Post it](../troop-reports/), or ask
someone else who trooped to.
