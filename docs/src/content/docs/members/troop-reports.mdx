One troop report is needed per troop, and anyone who trooped can post it. It's what counts
the troop towards everyone's [My Troops](../my-troops/).

## Post the report

<Steps>

1. After the event, open it and click **Create Troop Report**.

   <Image src={createButton} alt="Create Troop Report circled" />

2. Check the list of people against who came. Remove anyone who didn't, and add anyone who
   came off the waitlist.

   <Image src={reportForm} alt="The draft troop report" />

3. Fill in the weather, **Mission Report**, **Mission Status** and photographer.

4. Click **Post Troop Report**.

</Steps>

The report is posted to the troop reports forum, linked from the event's thread, and the
event is archived.

## Keep the roll call format

Leave each person's line as it is:

```
Ash - trooper1 - TK-20001
```

The username and Legion ID are how the troop is counted. If they're changed or removed,
that person isn't credited.

<Aside type="tip" title="Made a mistake?">
  Edit the report's first post. The troop is recounted from the edited version.
</Aside>

## Reminders

Until the report is posted, everyone who trooped gets a weekly **Troop Report Needed**
message.

Wranglers and waitlisted members can't post reports and don't get reminders.
