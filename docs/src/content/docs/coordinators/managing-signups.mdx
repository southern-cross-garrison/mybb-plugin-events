## Who's signed up

**Who is Attending** on the event lists everyone, with the waitlist underneath. Click
**Filter** to show one costume or one day.

For contact details, use the [attendance sheet](../attendance-sheet/).

## Remove someone

<Steps>

1. On the event, click **×** beside their name in **Who is Attending**.

2. A message to them is drafted. Add your reason.

   <Image src={removeDialog} alt="The Remove dialog with a drafted message" />

3. Click **Send PM & Remove**. Or use the arrow beside it to **Copy Text & Remove** (to send
   another way) or **Remove Without Notifying**.

</Steps>

This removes their whole signup, all days and roles. If there's a waitlist, the next
person gets the place.

## Removing the point of contact

Removing the point of contact's signup doesn't change who the point of contact is. The same
applies if they withdraw themselves, or if [removing a day](../multi-day-events/#removing-a-day)
cancels their signup. They:

- are still shown as **Point of Contact** on the event page
- can still open the [attendance sheet](../attendance-sheet/), with everyone's contact details
- are no longer listed on the attendance sheet, which only lists people signed up
- stay in the **Point of Contact** list on the event form, so editing something else
  doesn't remove them

To change the point of contact, [edit the event](../editing-events/) and choose someone else,
or **None**.

## Limits and the waitlist

Set **Maximum Troopers**, **Maximum Wranglers** or **Maximum Attendees** on the
[event form](../creating-events/#the-event-form). Once a limit is reached, new signups join a
waitlist.

- Places go in signup order. When someone drops out, the next person gets their place and
  is sent a private message.
- On multi-day events, each day has its own limit and waitlist.
- Waitlisted people are listed on the attendance sheet as **W1**, **W2** and so on, so you
  can call down the list on the day.

<Aside type="tip" title="Someone came off the waitlist on the day?">
  Add them to the troop report so their troop is counted. See
  [After the event](../after-the-event/).
</Aside>
