Excluded members can't see the event at all: it's not in their events list or calendar,
and they can't open its page or announcement thread. They aren't told.

<Steps>

1. [Edit the event](../editing-events/).

2. Under **Excluded Members**, type part of a username and pick the member from the list.

   <Image src={exclusions} alt="The Excluded Members field with a member added" />

3. Click **Save Changes**.

</Steps>

To un-exclude someone, click the **×** on their name and save.

- If they'd already signed up, their signup is withdrawn without a message. The save
  confirms how many signups were removed.
- They can still read the troop report after the event.
- Coordinators and admins can always see the event, even if excluded, but an excluded
  coordinator can't sign up.
