Open the event and click **View Attendance Sheet** at the bottom. The event's point of contact
can open it too.

<Image src={sheet} alt="The attendance sheet" />

It has one row per person, with their preferred name, username, role, Legion ID, WWCC
number, costumes, mobile number and emergency contact. Multi-day events have a **Days** column. Waitlisted
people are in a separate table, numbered **W1**, **W2** and so on.

## Print it

Press **Ctrl+P** (or **Cmd+P** on a Mac). It prints as a clean sheet, with the event
details at the top and the column headings repeated on every page.

Tick the **Attended** boxes on screen before printing, or leave them blank to tick by hand on
the day.

## Show one day

On a multi-day event, choose from **Day** to show only the people coming that day.

<Aside type="caution" title="Personal details">
  The sheet has members' phone numbers and emergency contacts. Keep printed copies safe,
  and dispose of them after the event.
</Aside>
