1. Open the event and click **Edit Event** at the bottom.

   <Image src={editButton} alt="Edit Event circled at the bottom of an event" />

2. Make your changes and click **Save Changes**.

</Steps>

The [announcement thread](../announcement-threads/) updates to match.

## Changes that affect signups

If a change would move people, the form lists who and asks you to confirm before saving:

- **Removing a day** removes those days from people's signups.
- **Lowering a limit** moves the latest signups back onto the waitlist.

Raising a limit gives places to people on the waitlist, in signup order. Anyone moved is
sent a private message from you.

You can't change **Type** once anyone has signed up.

<Aside title="Deleting an event">
  Coordinators can't delete events. Ask an administrator.
</Aside>
