<Steps>

1. Open the event and click **Edit Event** at the bottom.

   <Image src={editButton} alt="Edit Event circled at the bottom of an event" />

2. Make your changes and click **Save Changes**.

</Steps>

The [announcement thread](../announcement-threads/) updates to match.

## Changes that affect signups

Some changes move people and send them a private message from you. Saving the change sends
the message straight away, so check before you confirm.

| Change                                                        | What happens                                                             | Message sent                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Removing a day**, or moving a one-day event to another date | Everyone signed up for that day has their **whole signup cancelled**.    | **Event changed**, asking them to sign up again if the new dates suit them. |
| **Lowering a limit**                                          | The latest signups lose their place and go to the front of the waitlist. | **Moved to the waitlist**                                                   |
| **Raising a limit**                                           | People on the waitlist get places, in signup order.                      | **You have a place**                                                        |

For removing a day and lowering a limit, the form lists who'll be affected and asks you to
confirm. Raising a limit happens as soon as you save.

<Aside type="caution" title="Be careful with limits">
	Lowering a limit and then raising it again doesn't undo anything: people get a **Moved to the
	waitlist** message, then a **You have a place** message. Settle the limit before you save, and if
	the form lists people you didn't expect, change the limit back and save again instead of
	confirming.
</Aside>

Other changes, like a new start time, address or description, update the event and its
announcement thread but don't message anyone. Post in the thread if people need to know.

You can't change **Type** once anyone has signed up.

<Aside title="Deleting an event">Coordinators can't delete events. Ask an administrator.</Aside>
