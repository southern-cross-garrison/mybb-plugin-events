Go to **Admin CP** > **Event Management** > **Reports**. Coordinators with Admin CP access
can use it too.

<Image src={reports} alt="The People view of Reports" />

Reports count troops from posted [troop reports](../../members/troop-reports/). Events
without a report, and social events, aren't counted.

## Views

| View | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **People** | Troops per member as trooper and wrangler, their most worn costume and last troop. |
| **Events** | Each event's turnout against signups. |
| **Regions** | Troops, events and members per region. |
| **Costumes** | How often each costume was worn, and by how many members. |

Each view has summary cards, a month-by-month chart, and a top-ten chart above the table.

## Filter and sort

Click the bar above the view to set the **Order** (**Most first** or **Least first**),
**From** and **To** dates, **Region** and **Role**, then click **Show Report**.

**Least first** starts with members who've trooped once. Members who've never trooped aren't
listed.

## Nudge a member

Encourage a member who hasn't trooped lately to come back out.

<Steps>

1. In the **People** view, set **Order** to **Least first**, or filter to an earlier date
   range.

2. Click **Nudge…** beside the member.

3. Edit the drafted message, which mentions their last troop.

4. Click **Send PM**, or copy the message to send another way.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
  Fix a wrong count by editing the troop report's first post. The troop is recounted from
  the edited version.
</Aside>
