Go to **Admin CP** > **Event Management** > **Events**.

<Image src={list} alt="The Admin CP events list" />

The list shows every event with its status, type, region, start, signup counts and
coordinator. Coordinators with Admin CP access only see their own events here.

Click **Actions** on an event for:

| Option | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** | Opens the [event form](../../coordinators/creating-events/#the-event-form). |
| **View RSVPs** | Opens the event on the [RSVPs](../rsvps/) tab. |
| **Make Live** | Opens signups and posts the announcement thread. |
| **Archive** | See [Archive an event](#archive-an-event). |
| **Delete** | See below. |

To add an event, click **Add New Event**. It's the same form coordinators use on the forum.

<Aside title="Point of contact">
  Only you, the current point of contact and people with a place at the event can be
  chosen. See [Choosing a point of contact](../../coordinators/creating-events/#choosing-a-point-of-contact).
</Aside>

## Archive an event

Archiving closes out an event that has happened. Posting its troop report archives it
automatically. To archive it by hand:

<Steps>

1. Click **Actions** > **Archive** on the event.

   <Image src={archiveAction} alt="Archive circled in the event's Actions menu" />

</Steps>

Signups close, and the event no longer appears on the events list unless members tick
**Show archived**. Its page, signups and troop report are all kept.

If the board has an [archive forum](../../webmasters/settings/#set-up-an-archive-forum),
the event's announcement thread moves there, replies and all.

<Image src={archiveForum} alt="The archived event's thread circled in the Event Archive forum" />

To undo it, click **Actions** > **Make Live**. The thread moves back to its region's
forum.

## Delete an event

Deleting removes the event and everything attached to it:

- its signups and waitlist
- its **announcement thread and every reply**
- the troop counts from its report, from Reports and My Troops

The troop report thread itself stays in the troop reports forum.

<Aside type="caution">
  Deleting can't be undone. To close out an event that happened, archive it instead.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. Click **Actions** > **Delete** on the event.

2. Confirm.

</Steps>
