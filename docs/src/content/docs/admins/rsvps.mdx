---
title: RSVPs
description: See who's signed up to an event from the Admin CP.
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

Go to **Admin CP** > **Event Management** > **RSVPs**, or click **Actions** > **View
RSVPs** on an event.

Choose an **Event**, then optionally filter by **Costume** (matches part of a name) or
**Day**. Signups and the waitlist are listed separately.

For contact details and a printable roll, use the event's
[attendance sheet](../../coordinators/attendance-sheet/) on the forum.
