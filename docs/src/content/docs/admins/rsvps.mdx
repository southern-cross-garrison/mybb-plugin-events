<Steps>

1. Go to **Admin CP** > **Event Management** > **RSVPs**. Or, on the **Events** tab, click
   **Actions** > **View RSVPs** beside an event.

   <Image src={action} alt="The Actions menu open on an event, with View RSVPs circled" />

2. Choose an **Event**. To narrow the list, type part of a costume name in **Costume**, or
   choose a **Day**. Click **Apply Filters**.

   <Image src={filters} alt="The RSVP filters circled" />

3. Signups are listed first, then the waitlist in queue order.

   <Image src={list} alt="An event's RSVPs and waitlist" />

</Steps>

<Aside>
	For contact details and a printable roll, click **View Attendance Sheet** below the list. See [The
	attendance sheet](../../coordinators/attendance-sheet/).
</Aside>
