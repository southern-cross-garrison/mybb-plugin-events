Every event is filed under a region. Members filter the events list by region, and each
region can have its own forum for announcement threads.

Regions are at the bottom of **Admin CP** > **Event Management** > **Settings**.

## Add a region

<Steps>

1. Click **Add Region**, enter its name and save.

   <Image src={regions} alt="The region rows and the Add Region button circled" />

2. Choose its announcement forum on its row and click **Save Settings**.

</Steps>

## Rename a region

Edit its name and click **Save Settings**. Its events and announcement forum go with it.

## Delete a region

Click **×** at the end of its row. If it has events, choose which region to move them to.
There must always be at least one region.

<Image src={regionsDelete} alt="The delete dialog asking which region to move the events to" />

## Announcement forums

Each region's events are announced in the forum on its row. Regions set to **None** use the
**Default Event Forum**. If that isn't set either, their events aren't announced.

<Aside>
  Names can be up to 64 characters, with no commas or equals signs.
</Aside>

## Regions run by another unit

If members troop at a neighbouring garrison's events, you can cross-post those events
under a region of their own. Enter the other garrison's name in **Run by** on that
region's row, choose its logo (PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP or SVG, up to 1 MB), and click
**Save Settings**.

<Image src={regionsUnit} alt="A region's Run by box and logo circled" />

Members sign up to these events as usual. Each event shows the other garrison's logo and a
warning that signing up here doesn't sign them up with that garrison, with a link to the
real signup sheet. Coordinators must add that link when they create the event.

To replace the logo, choose another file. To remove it, tick **Remove**. If you clear
**Run by**, the region is yours again and its logo is removed.
